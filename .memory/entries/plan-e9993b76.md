Make semantopology_hardfork the canonical research engine, on its NumPy/HIPS physics backend, and reach the quality of the legacy semantopology_venice results.

Ten staged gates: (0) reconcile branches and repair the test suite, (1) thread discretization params into physics and restructure volume/projection ordering, (2) make the iteration loop fast, (3) Venice parity plus a deterministic regression baseline, (4) one canonical physical density as a measured change, (5) experiment interface, (6) semantic and sketch guidance, (7) blending and structural budget, (8) CNN + pixel residual, (9) harden and consolidate.

Two governing constraints discovered during verification:

1. Parity must precede semantic changes. Three different density fields are in play. Venice compliance sees sigmoid_with_constrained_mean, then scatter-to-zero, then cone filter. Venice CLIP sees the raw forward output expanded to RGB and resized, with no sigmoid, filter, mask, or clamp. Hardfork CLIP sees torch.sigmoid(logits) with a pre-blur. Routing CLIP through the physical density is therefore a third behavior, not a fix toward Venice, and must happen after parity is established or we lose the ability to attribute quality regressions.

2. Volume enforcement must move before Heaviside projection can exist. The cone filter is column-normalized in normalized_cone_filter_matrix, so it preserves total sum and volume survives filtering. Projection does not preserve the mean, so appending it to the current chain would violate volfrac silently.

Physics backend is settled as NumPy/HIPS. Commit bf64227, which deleted 313 lines of Torch solver, is titled 'Optimization for pure structure now converges as inteded over multigrid and runs fast'. A pyFANTOM backend was added in 98474b5 and deleted in dc09e57. The Torch solver used Jacobi-preconditioned CG capped at 400 iterations against a young_min=1e-9 to young=1.0 stiffness contrast that diagonal preconditioning cannot address. This is a performance question, not a capability one: StructuralLoss is a torch.autograd.Function whose backward runs autograd.grad through the full physics chain, so gradients are exact.

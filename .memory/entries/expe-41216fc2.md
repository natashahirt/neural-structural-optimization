Whole-elevation CLIP-only smoke test, prompt butterfly wing venation, 16 steps. Optimized a 16x8 control field, bilinearly decoded and smoothed to 32x64, then rank-cut occupancy to mean 0.25. High-frequency texture was strongly suppressed (neighbor contrast ratio 0.0907 vs 1.05 for direct pixels), and the prior strongly disagreed with the physics proxy (spatial_mass_loss 0.6074 vs >=0.25 gate). However, occupancy had 14 connected components and no top-to-bottom spanning component, so it failed the load-path geometry gate. Physics was not run. This separates two issues: low-dimensional control fixes grain, but CLIP's butterfly-wing signal still creates isolated large blobs/ridges rather than a continuous load path.
